Who we are

Safar North is a sample tour company made by a student team for our ICT class project. It shows how a traveller could plan a trip to Northern Pakistan online.

Our mission: make trip planning to the north simple and clear, so every family and student knows the cost before they travel.

Class project: not a registered company

Why travel with a local company?

  • Local guides know the roads, weather and safe seasons.
  • Money stays with local guest houses and drivers.
  • Help is close by if a road is blocked by a landslide.
  • Guides can share the culture and history of each valley.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to read the answer, or expand all at once.

Is this a real booking website?

No. It is a class demo. No booking is made and no money is taken.

Which cities can I travel from?

Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan and Karachi.

When is the best time to visit the north?

Mostly May to September. Roads to high places like Deosai and Babusar Top are often closed by snow in winter.

How is the trip cost worked out?

(Destination cost + package price + return coach fare) × number of travellers. Stays and gear are added on top.
